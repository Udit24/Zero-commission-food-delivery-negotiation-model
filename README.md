# Zero-commission-food-delivery-negotiation-model
A zero commission food delivery app where the delivery cost is negotiated between Restaurant, customer  and delivery person

# 🏛️ DontBite

A revolutionary, equitable food delivery ecosystem designed to break away from extractive, percentage-based commission models. Built on the core ethos of transparency, professional agency, and sustainable economics, this platform balances the incentives of Restaurateurs, Riders, and Customers through an algorithmic, relationship-driven marketplace.

---

## 💎 Features

*   **Three-Sided Price Discovery Mechanism**
    Dynamically balances real-time market forces between restaurants, riders, and customers to ensure optimal, fair pricing without centralized manipulation.

*   **Fixed-Tier Subscription for Restaurants**
    Restaurants pay a predictable flat rent based strictly on their physical or operational size in exchange for platform visibility. 
    *   **0% Revenue Commission:** No matter how high their sales volume climbs, the fee remains unchanged. Exceptional months do not penalize growth.
    *   **Stable Tier Transitions:** Restaurants are only transitioned to a higher payment slot if they demonstrate sustained, long-term sales growth over consistent quarters.

*   **Relationship-Driven Customer Delivery Fees**
    Customers pay a portion of the delivery fee negotiated directly with the restaurant. 
    *   **Persistent Relations:** Regular customers can establish a one-time negotiated rate with their favorite local eateries. 
    *   **Continuity:** This custom rate remains active indefinitely until the restaurant objects or the customer changes their delivery location.

*   **Fair Work, Fair Pay for Riders**
    Riders receive **100% of the negotiated delivery fee**. Earnings are calculated transparently using an equitable, multi-variable equation:
    Rider Pay = f(Base Fare, Distance, Time, Demand, Rider Availability, Rider Vote Count)

## 🧠 The Restaurateur Philosophy

This platform was engineered by analyzing systemic friction points within legacy delivery giants. We operate under four fundamental pillars derived from real-world operator experiences:

### 1. Revenue Protections over Extractive Commissions
> *"Restaurants do not hate paying money; they hate paying a percentage of their hard-earned revenue."*
Traditional platforms penalize efficiency and scaling. By treating platform access as digital real estate (rent) rather than taking a cut of the food, we preserve the thin margins of culinary businesses.

### 2. Dignified and Fair Compensation
Riders are the lifeblood of the logistics chain and deserve to be fairly compensated. By passing through the full delivery fee and factoring in qualitative metrics like peer/customer vote counts, we ensure professional sustainability for couriers.

### 3. Organic, Quid-Pro-Quo Pricing
Market dynamics shouldn't be governed by rigid, algorithmic fixed rates. Customers and restaurants thrive on organic pricing built on mutual value.
*   *Example:* If a historically loyal customer hasn't ordered in a while, the restaurant has the autonomy to completely waive their delivery fee, using the platform as a retention tool to proactively mend and nurture the relationship.

### 4. Non-Punitive Customer Relationship Management
Legacy systems use black-box algorithms where a single bad review can destroy a restaurant's visibility. This platform introduces a unique, collaborative customer database framework:
*   **The Redemption Principle:** One botched order due to peak-hour kitchen stress or courier mishaps shouldn't jeopardize an active customer relationship.
*   **Direct Resolution:** The system gives restaurants the data and the direct opportunity to rectify mistakes, offer concessions, and redeem themselves over time rather than enforcing punitive, make-or-break platform penalties.
